## Changelog
### Changes and additions:
  * Updated to DoLP version v0.641
  * Added bull pill effect (Only through spiking. Not available for purchase yet.)
  * Breeder pills now make you go into heat.
  * Breeder effect now prevents you from orgasming if you don't have any semen in your vagina.
  * Breeder effect now always has close to guaranteed chances of pregnancy.
  * Dressing feminine while considering yourself masculine is now stressful (if gender identity is enabled).
  * M2F effects may once again assign gentle demeanour to your face on first transformation.
  * Added Sissy/Butch pills to pharmacy.
  * May now purchase unknown pills from blackmarket seller at industrial alley (they work as if an NPC had spiked you).
  * Consecutive pill spikes may now stack effects on top of your current pill.
  * Added option to hide combat sprites when you're male or female (this should help when using combat image mods that showcase overly feminine or overly masculine sprites that wouldn't make sense when you're the opposite gender.)
