## Changelog
## New Content and Additions
* Added a new **Crop Progress** foldout under the **Jobs** statistics section.
  * The foldout lists all currently planted crops by:
    * Location.
    * Plot number.
    * Crop name.
    * Watering state.
    * Days remaining until harvest.
  * Exact harvest estimates are shown when blind stats are disabled.
  * Exact estimates are also shown if the player has enough tending skill or the **Green Thumb** background.
* Added a note to the start screen explaining that the gender identity meter may initially appear as non-binary regardless of selected settings.
  * This is because the meter is recalculated during the next daily update.
  * The note clarifies that the meter should correct itself after the first day passes.
* Added a **fingernail growth system**.
  * Fingernails now grow over time with four discrete stages:
    * Trimmed.
    * Neat.
    * Long.
    * Claw-like.
  * Fingernails reach full growth after around 14 in-game days.
  * Added a bathroom option to trim fingernails.
  * Added a player-facing fingernail description.
  * Fingernail descriptions now appear on the characteristics screen alongside the hair description.
* Added the **Scratch** combat action.
  * Scratch is a new action based on the player's fingernails or claws.
  * Scratch damage scales with the player's effective nail level.
  * Longer or sharper nails make Scratch more effective.
  * Existing nail-armour rings now correctly led to Scratch.
  * Long nails now apply a hand-skill penalty.
  * Cat transformation players always have Scratch available.
  * Cat transformation players do not suffer the long-nail hand-skill penalty.
  * Cat transformation players receive a unique idle description, describing their claws as sheathed beneath the pads.
  * Scratch could appear during consensual sex despite being a defiant action and is still hurting (a bit).
* Added **social and transformation integration** for fingernails.
  * Long nails now provide an attractiveness boost.
  * The mirror now includes a matching attractiveness breakdown entry.
  * Cat transformation players are exempt from the attractiveness boost, as their claws are hidden in social contexts.
  * Added a **Signature Manicure** treatment to the spa VIP wing.
  * Added five ambient fingernail flavour events across:
    * School hallway.
    * School courtyard.
    * Street.
* Expanded **NPC reactions to player scratching**.
   * Named NPCs now have distinct responses when the player scratches them.
    * Whitney treats the pain as amusing, provocative, and slightly enticing.
    * Pure Sydney reacts with pain, hurt, and loss of trust or love.
    * Corrupt Sydney becomes excited, unstable, and more dangerous, while still losing trust.
    * Robin reacts with emotional hurt, fear, trust loss, and may has optional minor trauma gain.
    * Leighton treats the scratch as insolence and escalates punishment.
    * Kylar frames the scratch as an obsessive marking moment, with stronger lust or rage with conditional love gain.
    * Harper treats the scratch as clinical evidence of aggression and non-compliance.
    * Gwylan ties scratches into marks, intimacy, heat, and restraint depending on context.
    * Eden reacts with panic and survival instincts tied to trauma, with love/trust loss and dominance loss.
    * Bailey frames the scratch as damage, debt, lowered value, and a reason for harsher control.
    * Generic reactions vary depending on enemy anger.
* Added the **Drunkenness Tolerance** trait.
  * Characters who get heavily drunk often will slowly build tolerance.
  * Higher tolerance makes drunkenness less tiring.
  * Alcohol leaves the body faster as tolerance rises, up to 1.5x speed.
  * At maximum tolerance, drunkenness no longer forces extra tiredness.
  * Tolerance decreases after staying sober.
  * It takes around 30 in-game days for tolerance to fully reset.
  * After long-term heavy drunk, suddenly going sober can cause withdrawal stress for the first 3 days.
  * The stronger the player's tolerance was, the stronger the withdrawal stress will be.
* Implemented **clover patches** as actual objects.
  * Clover can now be searched for real items.
  * This gives players a natural reason to interact with clover patches before discovering their Bunny transformation relevance.
  * Regular clover yield is affected by tending skill.
  * Four-leaf clovers are rare finds.
  * Regular clovers and four-leaf clovers are now declared as proper items.
  * Both clover types now have icon support.
  * Clovers can now be sold.
  * Clovers may now be requested by Gwylan.
  * Finding a four-leaf clover can award a feat.
* Added a **loft cooking-lessons** feature.
  * Every weekend, the player has a chance to teach the orphans how to cook.
  * This is only available after the kitchen loft has been unlocked.
  * If the player successfully teaches the orphans, they can begin using the kitchen to provide their own food.
  * The player can donate food to the orphans.
  * The player can also donate ingredients.
  * Donated food and ingredients may be eaten or used by the orphans for cooking.
  * The player can use their own donated ingredients to cook together with the orphans.
  * Cooking together with the orphans raises hope and rebelliousness (weekly).
* Expanded **DoLP speech attitude flavour text**.
  * Expanded related text across 12 files.
  * This gives different speech attitudes more distinct flavour and reduces repeated lines.
* Added missing **snow storm** and **thunderstorm** icons.

## Rebalancing and Mechanics
* Rebalanced **pain recovery and escape difficulty**.
  * By vinmis / Astalsi401.
  * Pain now takes more time to recover from.
  * Players should be more careful when deciding whether to fight back while in pain.
  * Tiredness now increases more depending on pain.
  * Being penetrated now increases pain regardless of how experienced the player character is.
  * In the Mines, Livestock, Underground Brothel, and wolf cave, digging to escape can now be affected by pain and tiredness.
  * This makes escape attempts more sensitive to the player's physical condition.
* Improved pain, tiredness, and escape helper reliability.
  * Bug fix by D.N.A.
  * Several combat helper functions were moved into the shared `setup` namespace.
  * These helpers now work more consistently across JavaScript and Twee files.
  * This prevents load-order issues where helpers could work in one file but fail in another.
  * Fixed a body-size pain modifier issue.
  * Newer body size values could previously cause incorrect or missing pain calculations.
  * Updated affected combat, time, player option, and escape-related call sites to use the new shared helpers.
  * There is no intended balance change for normal body sizes.
  * Escape difficulty behaviour is unchanged aside from the reliability fixes.
* Removed RNG from the **streetMoneyProposition** event.
  * The event should now behave more consistently instead of relying on random chance in that section.

## Fixes
* Fixed **Winter tug horn check**.
  * Fixed an issue where the check could behave incorrectly when the player had no horns.
* Fixed **same-turn virginity co-taker crediting**.
  * Fixed virginity loss crediting when multiple attackers penetrate the same orifice during one combat turn.
  * Previously, the first attacker to process would call `takeVirginity` and change the player's virginity state away from true.
  * Later attackers in the same turn would then enter the non-virgin branch.
  * This caused later attackers to skip virginity credit entirely.
  * As a result, only one taker could appear in stats even when multiple attackers were involved in the same turn.
  * Later attackers in the same turn can now be credited correctly.
* Fixed **drugged scream suppression**.
  * Fixed an issue where any non-zero `$drugged` value could trigger the scream-suppression branch.
  * This included tiny leftover decay values that should not have mattered.
  * The check now uses a willpower-scaled threshold:
    * `$drugged gte (currentSkillValue('willpower') / 10)`
  * This matches the nearby ear-slime threshold logic.
  * Negligible drugged values should no longer convert screams into moans.
* Fixed an **NPC slot index off-by-one** issue in the water dragon elder event.
  * Fix by CitrusBurst.
* Fixed **Poppy speech grammar**.
  * Fix by Sei / Karl Sendis.
* Fixed a crash in the **hairdresser**.
* Fixed the **radio**.
  * Added the missed storm case.
* Fixed **X-Change Choice**.
  * X-Change Choice now only runs a fresh gender flip if the player is not already X-Changed in any way.
  * This covers players who are:
    * Pilled with a gender-flipping pill.
    * Pilled with a non-flipping pill, such as cumcure or innocence.
    * Already pilled through the trans-pre-pilled feature.
  * The old check only looked for `!$xchange.pill.genderflipEffect`.
  * This allowed the inner block to run even when the player was already X-Changed through a non-flipping pill or trans-pre-pilled state.
  * That block could call `xchange-effects` and overwrite `$xchange.pill.baseSex` with the current `$player.sex`.
  * This could flip the intended trans X-Change direction.
  * It could also feminise or masculinise the player back into their birth body.
  * The new check prevents X-Change Choice from resetting or stomping existing X-Change state.
* Fixed **Brothel Exclusive Gloryhole**.
  * Fixed issues affecting the exclusive brothel gloryhole scene.
* Fixed **sides haircut target lengths**.
  * Sides haircut options now land in their intended hair length stages.
  * Previously, some options ended up one stage too long.
  * Fixed the 100-150 hair length dead zone where the ears option became unavailable.
  * Fringe logic was checked and left unchanged because its offset is intentional.
* Fixed **Whitney Street Bully Chocolate**.
  * Fixed an issue where the scene did not treat the kiss as taking virginity even though virginity loss had occurred.
* Fixed **X-Change Whitney**.
  * Fixed an issue where willingly getting spiked in the alleyway could incorrectly lead to the hallway.
