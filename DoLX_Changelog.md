## Changelog

## Changes and Additions

- **Expanded starting sex toy choices**

  - You can now start with any existing toy, including forest-shop toys and novelty chastity devices.
  - Novelty devices start in your wardrobe together with their keys.
  - Colour selection and random picks include the new choices.

- **Sewinator-9000 repairs now use spools**

  - Repairing clothing costs 1 spool for each damaged piece. Undamaged clothes cost nothing.

- **Perfume now raises beauty**

  - Wearing perfume gives half the beauty bonus of a fresh bath. It lasts until the scent fades or washes off.

- **Being sold at the Flats auction now leads to the new vanilla mine**
  - The auction always sends you to the v0.5.13.6 mine. This makes Winter's mine antique and the mine clothing at the forest shop obtainable.
  - The old guards' quarters encounters now happen during the mine's cleaning job. Guards may make you climb onto a table or lead you around on a cord.
  - Refusing the guards can end badly. Fighting back can let you escape in the lift.
  - Pretending to clean may reveal a hidden tunnel. Dig through it to escape into the storm drains.
  - Passing out while cleaning can lead to the minecart scene in the forgotten tunnels.
  - Saves made inside the old guards' quarters now load at the start of the new mine.

## Fixes and Improvements

- **Fixed bedtime outfit lists showing clothes that are not sleepwear**

  - Joining Avery in bed now opens the right wardrobe and takes you to the right bed.

- **Fixed a recipe missing from the Journal**

- **Fixed the pregnancy settings layout and pregnancy length display**

  - Fixed broken spacing and removed stray HTML code from the pregnancy options.
  - The wolf pregnancy length shown now updates when you move its slider.

- **Fixed the settings export buttons and the NPC skin tone preview when importing settings**

- **Moved save recovery tools into a new Backups tab**

  - The Saves tab keeps the vanilla layout and the Updated UI styling.
  - Storage advice and APK recovery copies are now in Backups. Warnings about real save loss still show on the Saves tab.

- **Fixed a missing or unreachable link**

- **Fixed Robin's stand upgrades taking your money without applying the upgrade**

- **Fixed freckles disappearing while blushing with custom image packs**

  - Blush is drawn over the mouth again.
  - Custom blusher colours now stay when you have freckles, and padded blush sprites display correctly.

- **Fixed repeated text in Hopeless Cycle and in clothing previews**

- **Fixed a permanent hunger lockout at Remy's farm**
  - Breastfeeding no longer breaks the hunger and thirst meters.
  - Affected saves are repaired, so the normal farm choices become available again.
