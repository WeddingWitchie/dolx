## Changelog
## Changes and Additions:
* Added **shady dealer rumours** to the journal.
  * Hearing rumours about shady dealers in the school hallways now lets the player know where they can be found that week.
  * Their current weekly location is now recorded in the journal.
* Updated the **cheat menu**.
  * Added controls for X-Change pill duration.
  * Pill effects can now be set to last for 1 day, 3 days, 1 week, 1 month, or permanently.
* Added a new **horse race betting minigame**.
  * Unlocks from the farm once the farm has been fully cleared.
  * Players can visit the racecourse, choose a horse to back, place a bet, and either watch the race in real time or skip straight to the result.
  * The race display shows horses moving around the track.
  * Includes 2 race field types.
  * Races use 3 to 5 random horses.
  * Each horse has betting odds.
  * Horses can trigger random skills during the race.
  * Skill activations are highlighted in the race log.
  * Track going is based on the current weather.
  * Winning bets pay out according to the chosen horse’s odds.
  * The system takes inspiration from Umamusume-style horse racing while fitting into the farm and DoL passage flow.
* Added an option to choose your own **transformation description**.
  * Available in the mirror under **Examine your inner self**.
  * Lets players pick the title/description used for their current transformation where valid.
* Added an option to turn **NPC voiceovers** on or off.
  * NPC moaning and orgasm sounds can now be enabled or disabled.
* Updated **Water Dragon transformation** content.
  * Water Dragon now has its own claw/scratch text.
  * Uses the same claw rebalance as the other animal transformations.
* Updated **Jordan**.
  * Jordan may now react to the player’s divine transformation when spoken to at the temple.
* Updated the **content guide** and global DoLX repository description.
* Updated credits.
