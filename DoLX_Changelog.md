## Changelog
## Changes and Additions:
* Reworked **nails/animal claws**.
  * Cat, wolf, fox, and bear transformations can now all use claws properly.
  * Scratch/claw text now better reflects the player’s current animal traits.
  * Smaller characters now deal more damage with scratches/claws in dangerous fights.
  * Larger, stronger characters still benefit more from punching, so claws do not replace strength builds (no buf).
  * Consensual scenes keep scratch/claw damage low, so playful scratching does not end scenes too quickly.
  * Animal claws currently share the same damage balance, but each type has different flavour text.

## Rebalancing and Fixes
* Rebalanced **passive pain recovery**.
  * Pain now slowly decreases every minute while the player is hurt.
  * Pain recovery starts gently at 0.3 pain per minute.
  * Pain recovery gradually increases over time, up to a maximum of 0.5 pain per minute.
  * The longer the player remains continuously in pain, the more their body acclimates to it.
  * Pain adaptation improves in stages after 1 hour, 3 hours, 7 hours, 15 hours, 31 hours, and further stages.
  * Pain recovery is still capped and will never decay faster than the maximum allowed rate.
